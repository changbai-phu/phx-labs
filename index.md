# Welcome to pHX Labs

pHX Labs is my personal space for exploring ideas, building projects, and documenting what I learn along the way.

This site collects things I find interesting to build, study, or write about — ranging from machine learning systems and automation workflows to simulations, research notes, and occasional technical deep dives.

Much of my work sits at the intersection of machine learning, automation, and system design. Through this space, I experiment with end-to-end ML projects, workflow automation, simulations, and other technical ideas worth investigating.

Rather than focusing on a single domain, pHX Labs serves as a growing archive of projects, technical notes, and experiments accumulated over time.
